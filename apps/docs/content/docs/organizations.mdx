# Organizations

An organization gives its members a shared workspace. Your membership determines which workspaces and organization settings you can access.

## Create or find an organization

Open **Organizations** from the workspace switcher, or go to `/organizations`. Search the list by organization name or slug, then select an organization to open its workspace.

Choose **New organization** in the list or workspace switcher. Enter a name and select **Create**. The service creates the address slug and opens the new workspace under your owner account. Verify your email before creating an organization. Names must contain 1–100 characters. Don't use leading or trailing spaces or control characters. The service skips reserved or unavailable slugs when it creates the address.

If the page says another creation already runs for your account, wait for it to finish before trying again. If creation still fails, try again from **Organizations**.

## Workspace and navigation

A workspace URL starts with its organization slug, for example `/<organization-slug>/home`. Use the sidebar switcher to move among organizations you belong to, open **All organizations**, or create another organization. The workspace includes Home, Projects, Templates, and Settings. You need organization membership to open its workspace.

## Members and invitations

Open **Settings → Members** to see the roster. The list shows each member's name, email, and role. Owners and admins can invite someone by email. The invitation assigns the **member** role after acceptance and expires after 48 hours.

The invited person should open the link in the invitation email and sign in or create an account with that email address. They must verify their email before they can accept. If the page shows a different signed-in email, select **Switch account** and sign in with the invited address. After the person accepts, the app opens the organization's workspace.

Owners and admins can see pending invitations, select **Resend** to start a new 48-hour period, or cancel an invitation. If a link expires or becomes unavailable, ask an owner or admin to select **Resend**.

## Roles and permissions

- **Owner**: the creator becomes the owner and can manage organization settings and invitations.
- **Admin**: can manage organization settings and invitations.
- **Member**: can access the workspace and view the member roster.

The member screen doesn't offer role changes or member removal. Invitations from this screen always assign the member role.

## If something doesn't work

- If an organization doesn't appear, confirm that you signed in to the expected account. Search by its name or slug. The list includes only organizations you belong to.
- If you can't open a workspace or manage its settings, confirm that your account belongs to the organization. Settings and pending invitations require an owner or admin role.
- If you can't create an organization, verify your email and check the organization name rules. Wait for any creation already running for your account to finish.
- If you can't accept an invitation, check that the link remains valid, sign in with the invited email, and verify that address. Ask an owner or admin to select **Resend** if the invitation expired or became unavailable.
