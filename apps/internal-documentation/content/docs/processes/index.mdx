A process is a procedure that runs on demand: a release, a hotfix, a roll-out, a smoke test. Each page in this section documents one process end-to-end, with its triggers, prerequisites, steps, artefacts, and recovery paths.

Processes live here when they are read by people **executing** a task at the moment of execution. Decisions live in [Decisions](../decisions/index.mdx); standing constraints live in [Rules](../rules/index.mdx); study-of-a-library content lives in the [Knowledge Base](../knowledge-base/index.mdx).

The full set lives at `documents/internal/process/`.

## Pages in this section

- [Canary releases](./canary-releases.mdx), publishing `@deessejs/cli@canary` on demand via `workflow_dispatch`.
- [PR previews](./pr-previews.mdx), per-PR ephemeral preview packages via pkg.pr.new.
